💳 How cashless treatment works

  1. Go to an APA network hospital or clinic.
  2. Tell reception: “I have APA Health Insurance and would like to use cashless treatment.”
  3. Show your APA insurance card/policy information and ID.
  4. The facility checks your eligibility with APA.
  5. APA pays eligible covered costs directly up to your policy limits. You pay excluded or excess amounts.

🤒 Normal sickness

For fever, flu, sore throat, stomach problems, headache, minor infection, and similar issues, start with an APA network clinic or hospital and ask for cashless service.

🛵 Accident

For minor accident treatment without admission, your emergency OPD accident limit applies. For serious injury, head injury, severe bleeding, breathing difficulty, or suspected fracture, seek emergency care first.

⚠️ Important

Network hospital does not mean everything is free. Cashless treatment applies only to eligible treatment within your policy terms and limits. Some care may require approval, may exceed a sub-limit, or may not be covered.

🎒 What to bring

APA insurance card/policy information · ID/passport · phone · some backup money in case part of the treatment is not covered.
